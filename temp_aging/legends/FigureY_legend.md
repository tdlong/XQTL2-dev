**Supplementary Figure Y | Nine cages predict the tenth.** **a**, Predicted
against observed change in founder frequency at the ten highest-ranked positions,
for every held-out cage and all four treatments. Positions are ranked and weights
estimated on the nine training cages, and the held-out cage's selected proportion
is known, so each prediction is made without any parameter fitted to the cage it
is tested in. The dashed line is the line of identity, not a regression; $r$ and
the regression slope are inset. Colours are the eight founders, as in Figure 2.
**b**, Heritability explained by a score built on the top $N$ markers, with
markers ranked on the nine training cages and the correlation between them
divided out. Shaded bands are jackknife standard errors over the ten folds. The
dashed line is the marker-based heritability of the whole genome — the
counterpart of SNP-based heritability for this population — estimated from the
same data; it is not well determined, because the eight founders recombine into
roughly 55 independent segments and a variance that coarse cannot be pinned from
ten replicate cages.
