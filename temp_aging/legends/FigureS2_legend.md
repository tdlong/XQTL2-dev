Power against experiment size for four designs, at a locus explaining 2% of
phenotypic variance (left) and 0.5%, the size resolved here (right). The x axis
is individuals phenotyped, or lines for the inbred-line and RIL panels. Solid
segments span the sizes these experiments are currently run at; dotted segments
are hypothetical extensions that nobody has run. Points mark each design as
currently practised, and the dashed horizontal line is 80% power.
